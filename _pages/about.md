    <h1 id="intro-title">Soroush Esfahanian</h1>
    <p class="hero-affiliation">Research Intern, <a href="https://nlp.epfl.ch/">EPFL NLP Lab</a><br>Lausanne, Switzerland</p>
    <p class="intro">I want to understand how language models reason: the internal mechanisms behind their answers, how those mechanisms emerge through training, and why they sometimes fail.</p>
    <p>I am a final-year Computer Engineering student at the <a href="https://ut.ac.ir/en">University of Tehran</a> and a research intern at <a href="https://www.epfl.ch/">EPFL</a>, supervised by <a href="https://atcbosselut.github.io/">Prof. Antoine Bosselut</a>. My current work investigates how distillation configurations affect the reasoning capabilities transferred from teacher to student models.</p>
    <p>I am interested in the internal computations that support reasoning, and how pretraining and post-training change them. I would like to use mechanistic interpretability and controlled experiments to understand these processes, including whether a model’s written reasoning reflects how it reaches an answer. I am also interested in connections with human cognition, especially reasoning about beliefs and intentions.</p>
    <p>Previously, at <a href="https://teias.institute/">TeIAS</a>, I co-developed <a href="#publications">PerSo</a>, a benchmark for cognitive and behavioral social intelligence in LLMs. Earlier, I worked on learning-based sound source localization at the University of Tehran’s <a href="https://taarlab.com/">Human and Robot Interaction Lab</a>.</p>
