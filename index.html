Soroush Esfahanian

Research Intern, EPFL NLP Lab
Lausanne, Switzerland

I am a final-year Computer Engineering student at the University of Tehran and a research intern at EPFL, supervised by Prof. Antoine Bosselut. My current work investigates how distillation configurations affect the reasoning capabilities transferred from teacher to student models.

Previously, at TeIAS, I co-developed PerSo, a benchmark for cognitive and behavioral social intelligence in LLMs. Earlier, I worked on learning-based sound source localization at the University of Tehran’s Human and Robot Interaction Lab.

Research

EPFL · NLP Lab

Aug 2026–present

Research Intern · Prof. Antoine Bosselut

Studying how distillation configurations shape reasoning and student behavior in language models. My work compares rollout horizons, prefix sources, and KL directions through checkpoint evaluations, and examines answer-format alignment, reasoning loops, and recovery during training.

TeIAS · LLM Lab

Jun 2025–Jul 2026

Researcher · Dr. Mohammad Taher Pilehvar and Dr. Yadollah Yaghoobzadeh

Co-developed PerSo, a benchmark evaluating cognitive and behavioral social intelligence in LLMs through real-world persuasion scenarios. Designed belief, emotion, intent, and persuasion-response tasks, built the dataset with LLM-assisted generation and human validation, and evaluated 15 model configurations across five families.

University of Tehran · Human and Robot Interaction Lab

2024

Researcher · Dr. Mehdi Tale Masouleh

Developed learning-based 3D sound source localization using audio timing and spectral features. Collected audio at 315 locations and evaluated multi-branch neural networks and region classifiers. Published and presented the work at ICRoM 2024.
